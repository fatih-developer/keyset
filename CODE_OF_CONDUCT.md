# Code of Conduct

Keyset is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants must act professionally, assume good faith while resolving
disagreements, and avoid personal attacks, discrimination, harassment, sexual
content directed at others, doxxing, threats, and knowingly disruptive conduct.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when conduct violates this policy. Reports should be made privately to the
repository maintainers through the contact method documented in `SECURITY.md`
when the report involves safety or sensitive information. Do not include
credentials, tokens, private keys, or environment files in any report.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
events, and other spaces where a participant represents the Keyset project.
